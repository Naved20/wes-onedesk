# 🎯 Quick Guide: Remark Rating Radio Buttons

## What Changed?

### BEFORE (Stars):
```
💪 Confidence (आत्मविश्वास)                    5/5
★ ★ ★ ★ ★
(Click on stars)
```

### AFTER (Radio Buttons):
```
💪 Confidence (आत्मविश्वास)
○ 1 ★    ○ 2 ★    ○ 3 ★    ○ 4 ★    ● 5 ★
(Select radio button)
```

---

## How to Use

### Step 1: Open Remark Dialog
Click "Add Remark" button on any task response

### Step 2: Enter Remark Text
Type your feedback in the textarea

### Step 3: Select Ratings
For each category, click the radio button next to your rating:

```
💪 Confidence (आत्मविश्वास)
○ 1 ★    ○ 2 ★    ○ 3 ★    ● 4 ★    ○ 5 ★
                              ↑
                         Click here for 4
```

### Step 4: Review Overall Rating
See the average automatically calculated:
```
Overall Average Rating: 4/5 ★★★★
```

### Step 5: Submit
Click "Add Remark" button

---

## All 5 Categories

1. 💪 **Confidence** (आत्मविश्वास)
2. 📚 **Vocabulary** (शब्दावली)
3. 🎵 **Tone** (स्वर)
4. 👋 **Hand Gesture** (हाथ के इशारे)
5. ⚡ **Speed** (गति)

Each has 5 radio buttons: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5
