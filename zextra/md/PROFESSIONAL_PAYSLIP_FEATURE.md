## 🎓 Understanding Your Payslip

### Gross Earnings
- Fixed salary earned based on attendance
- Plus variable earnings (bonuses, incentives)
- Total amount before deductions

### Deductions
- EPF: Retirement savings (12% of basic)
- ESIC: Insurance benefit (0.75% of gross)
- TDS: Tax deducted at source
- Professional Tax: State tax
- Other: Any additional deductions

### Net Salary
- Gross earnings minus all deductions
- Amount you receive in your bank account
- Your take-home pay

### Employer Contributions
- EPF Employer: Company's contribution to your retirement
- ESIC Employer: Company's insurance contribution
- NOT deducted from your salary
- Added to calculate CTC

### CTC (Cost to Company)
- Total value of your compensation
- Includes salary and employer benefits
- Shows true cost to company
- Useful for understanding total package
