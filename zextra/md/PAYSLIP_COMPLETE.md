### 1. Employee Information
- Name

- Department
- Salary Period
- Status (Locked/Draft)
- Approval Status

### 2. Attendance Details
- Working Days
- Present Days
- Paid Leaves
- Absent Days
- Total Paid Days

### 3. Earnings Breakdown
- Fixed Gross Salary
- Per Day Rate Calculation
- Gross Earned
- Fixed Components (Basic, HRA, Other)
- Variable Earnings
- Total Gross

### 4. Deductions Breakdown
- EPF (Employee Provident Fund)
- ESIC (Employee State Insurance)
- TDS (Tax Deducted at Source)
- Professional Tax
- Other Deductions
- Total Deductions

### 5. Net Salary
- Gross Earnings
- Less: Deductions
- **Net Salary (Take Home)**

### 6. Employer Contributions
- EPF Employer Contribution
- ESIC Employer Contribution
- Total Employer Contribution

### 7. CTC (Cost to Company)
- Gross Earnings
- Plus: Employer Contributions
- **Total CTC**

### 8. Summary Table
- Quick reference of all amounts
- Easy comparison
