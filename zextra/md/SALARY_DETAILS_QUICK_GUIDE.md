## 💡 Understanding Your Salary

### How It's Calculated

1. **Fixed Gross Salary** (Monthly)
   - Example: ₹10,000/month

2. **Per Day Rate**
   - ₹10,000 ÷ 26 working days = ₹384.62/day

3. **Earned Amount**
   - ₹384.62 × 15 paid days = ₹5,769.30

4. **Add Variable Earnings**
   - Lesson Plan: ₹500
   - Training: ₹300
   - Total Gross: ₹6,569.30

5. **Subtract Deductions**
   - EPF: -₹300
   - ESIC: -₹43.50
   - TDS: -₹500
   - Total Deductions: -₹843.50

6. **Net Salary (Take Home)**
   - ₹6,569.30 - ₹843.50 = **₹5,725.80**

7. **Add Employer Benefits** (Not deducted)
   - EPF Employer: +₹300
   - ESIC Employer: +₹188
   - Total CTC: **₹6,213.80**

## ❓ FAQ

**Q: Why is my net salary less than gross?**
A: Deductions like EPF, ESIC, TDS, and taxes are subtracted from gross to get net.

**Q: What's the difference between Net and CTC?**
A: Net is what you receive. CTC includes employer contributions (benefits) not deducted from your salary.

**Q: Why does my salary vary each month?**
A: Because it's based on your attendance. More present days = higher salary.

**Q: What is EPF?**
A: Employee Provident Fund - a retirement savings scheme. Both you and employer contribute.

**Q: What is ESIC?**
A: Employee State Insurance - provides medical and disability benefits.

**Q: Can I download my payslip?**
A: Coming soon! PDF download feature will be available.

**Q: Can I see past months?**
A: Yes! You can select different months to view historical salary data.
